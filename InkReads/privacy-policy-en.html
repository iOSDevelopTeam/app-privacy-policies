InkReads Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026

Welcome to InkReads (the “App”), a local-first reading and note-taking app. All your content is stored on your device. The App has no account system or servers of its own and uses no third-party analytics or usage statistics services. This policy explains the data the App actually handles, how it handles it, and your control over it, in line with the current version’s behavior.

1. Data handled by the App (stored locally on your device)

1.1 Content you create

  • Notes: Text, images, tags, and associated book information you create.
  • Books: Book metadata you add (title, author, reading status, rating, and progress), covers, and imported local files (PDF, EPUB, TXT, HTML, and Markdown).
  • Topic notebooks: Topic notebooks you organize on this device, including local copies of excerpts and thoughts.
  • Reading bookmarks: Reading positions you add in local books.
  • Sharing history: Records saved on your device after you generate quote cards.
  • Templates and settings: Card templates, fonts, themes, notifications, and other preferences.

This data is stored locally using Apple’s Core Data framework and the App’s documents directory. It is shared within the App through an App Group directory, for example for reading progress. This data is not uploaded to any server.

1.2 Information the App does not collect

  • The App does not collect device identifiers, IP addresses, or location information.
  • The App does not collect usage statistics such as frequency of use, time spent, or feature usage.
  • The App contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or crash reporting SDKs.
  • The App has no servers of its own or technical channel for uploading your data.

1.3 Diagnostic information collected by Apple’s system mechanisms

  • Aggregate quality metrics: Apple collects aggregate metrics such as crashes and hangs through system mechanisms (MetricKit). The developer can only view aggregate information such as counts and versions in App Store Connect. These metrics do not include note text, images, book files, or backup content. The App does not forward them to any other server.
  • Issue reports: Only when you use the App’s feedback feature and choose to include diagnostics does the App generate a diagnostic summary you can preview line by line (app version, build version, system version, device category, and aggregate quality counts). You can review all content and cancel before sending. You choose how to send it through the system share sheet; the App does not send it automatically.

1.4 Permissions

The App requests these permissions only when you actively use the corresponding feature:

  • Camera: Take a photo for a book cover or card background.
  • Photos: Choose book covers, note images, or card backgrounds, and save cards to Photos.
  • Notifications: Reading reminders. You can turn them off in system settings at any time.

The App does not request other permissions such as location, microphone, contacts, or health access.

2. How data is used

Data is used only to provide features on your device: creating and editing notes, local reading and saving progress, organizing topic notebooks, displaying statistics, generating quote cards, and exporting and restoring backups. All processing takes place on your device, without behavioral profiling, tracking, or personalized advertising.

3. Storage and retention

  • Storage locations: Local Core Data storage (in the App Group directory), the documents directory (note images and local book files), the Application Support directory (topic notebooks), and system preferences (lightweight settings and reading bookmarks).
  • Backups: You can export your data as a JSON backup file and store it yourself. A complete backup includes books, notes, templates, images, bookmarks, and topic notebooks. Restoring a backup replaces all data. The App automatically creates a snapshot before restoring and restores the original data if the operation fails.
  • Retention: Data stays on your device until you delete the relevant content, use the App’s Clear Data feature, or uninstall the App. Exported backup files are not affected by uninstalling the App and remain under your control.

4. Sharing information

The App does not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties. The only exception is where required by law, regulation, or legal proceedings, and only to the extent necessary.

5. Your rights

  • View and edit: View, edit, and delete your notes, books, bookmarks, and topic notebooks in the App at any time.
  • Export: Create a complete backup through Settings → Data Management → Export Data.
  • Delete: Delete individual items or use Clear Data to remove all local data at once.
  • Account deletion: The App has no account system, so no account deletion is needed. Uninstalling the App deletes its data from your device.

6. Children’s privacy

The App is not marketed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect children’s personal information. The App has no technical capability to collect any user information. Parents with concerns may clear the data or uninstall the App at any time.

7. Policy updates

If the App’s behavior changes, especially if any data collection, uploading, or third-party sharing is added, we will update this policy and the privacy manifest (PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy) before release and state the effective date. Please review this policy periodically.

8. Contact us

If you have questions about this policy or the App’s handling of data, please contact:

  • Email: inkreads@163.com

We will respond within 30 days of receiving your request.

Thank you for reading this policy. InkReads’ privacy commitment is simple: Your data belongs to you and stays on your device.

Support

Answers for App Store Connect Support URL visitors—and anyone using Hanlo offline.

Hanlo is a structured Mandarin learning app for iPhone. Lessons, audio, and review run on your device. Need help? Email inkreads@163.com.

FAQ

Does Hanlo work offline?

Yes. All 12 micro-lessons and bundled native-standard MP3 audio ship with the app. You can study on flights and subways with no internet. Optional course-pack downloads (if offered later) are for content delivery only—not for uploading your voice.

Is my microphone audio uploaded?

No. Tone Lab and speak-aloud practice estimate pitch on your iPhone. Voice samples stay in memory on device and are not sent to Hanlo servers or third-party speech APIs. You can deny microphone permission in iOS Settings; listening and reading still work. See the Privacy Policy for full detail.

Simplified vs Traditional Chinese — and stroke order

You can switch between Simplified and Traditional character display in Profile. Hanzi Workshop stroke-order tracing currently follows the simplified form even when Traditional display is on. We call this out in-app so practice stays honest.

How do I reset local progress?

In the app, open Profile → Privacy → Reset Local Progress. This erases local XP, streak, FSRS history, completed lessons, and onboarding placement on that device. Because there is no cloud account, deleting the app also removes local learning state.

Why do I hear pre-recorded audio instead of system TTS?

Hanlo uses teacher-checked, pre-generated offline MP3s (including 0.75× and 1× dialogue speeds). There is no live system text-to-speech for lesson lines.

Is Hanlo free? Do I need an account?

v1 is free. No account is required. There are no third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.

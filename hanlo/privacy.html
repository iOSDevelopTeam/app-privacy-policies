Hanlo Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 23, 2026 · Last Updated: November 23, 2026
Core promise: Hanlo is an offline-first Mandarin learning app. Microphone audio used for tone practice is processed entirely on your device. We do not upload your voice recordings to third-party AI or cloud speech servers. No account is required. We do not use third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.

1. Who We Are

This Privacy Policy explains how the Hanlo iOS application (“Hanlo”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use the app. Contact: inkreads@163.com.

2. Information We Store

2.1 Data stored only on your device

Hanlo keeps learning state locally (on-device SQLite / UserDefaults), including:

  • Lesson progress, XP, streak, daily goal, and placement / onboarding choices
  • FSRS spaced-repetition schedules and review history
  • Tone drill results and temporary pitch analysis in memory
  • Handwriting practice comparisons and bookmarked / mastered characters
  • Display preferences (e.g. Traditional Chinese, speech speed, silent mode, haptics)
  • Downloaded offline course packs and bundled lesson audio / assets

This information stays on your iPhone unless you choose to export or share it yourself (for example via the in-app JSON export share sheet).

2.2 What we do not collect

  • No account, email, or phone number is required to use Hanlo
  • No personal profile is created on our servers for learning progress
  • No third-party analytics, advertising, or cross-app tracking SDKs
  • No sale of personal information
  • Apple’s privacy nutrition labels / PrivacyInfo: tracking is disabled; no collected data types are declared for network collection

3. Microphone & Audio

Microphone access is requested only when you start a recording action (for example Tone Lab or role-play speaking practice). Audio is used to:

  • Estimate pitch contour (F0) locally for tone comparison
  • Optionally play back your recording to you on the same device

Voice samples are processed in memory on device and are not uploaded to Hanlo servers or third-party AI / speech APIs. You can deny microphone permission in iOS Settings; non-speaking features continue to work.

4. Network Use

Hanlo is designed to work offline with bundled and downloaded course content. If / when the app contacts our CDN or API (for example to fetch course packs), those requests are for content delivery—not for uploading your microphone recordings or selling personal data. Crash or diagnostics tooling is not enabled in the current shipping build covered by this policy.

5. Purchases (if offered)

If subscriptions or in-app purchases are offered, payment is processed by Apple. We do not receive your full payment card details. Apple may share limited subscription status with us solely to unlock purchased features.

6. Your Controls (GDPR / CCPA & similar)

Inside the app under Profile → Privacy you can:

  • Export Learning Data (JSON) — download a copy of local progress fields
  • Reset Local Progress — erase local XP, streak, FSRS history, completed lessons, and onboarding placement on this device

Because learning data lives on device and no cloud account is required, deleting the app (or resetting progress) removes that local learning state. For privacy questions or requests, email inkreads@163.com.

7. Children’s Privacy

Hanlo is a language-learning tool and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

8. International Users

Hanlo is built for learners worldwide. Local device storage remains on your device. If you contact support by email, the information you include in that email will be processed to respond to your request.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the product changes. The “Last Updated” date at the top will be revised, and material changes will be reflected on this page at https://justdoios.top/hanlo/privacy.
